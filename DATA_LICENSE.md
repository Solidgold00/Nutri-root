# NutriRoot V0.4 data rights and attribution

NutriRoot's code and its nutrition data do not automatically share the same usage rights. The runtime catalogue at `backend/data/catalog.json` contains nutrient values taken from, or calculated using ingredients from, the **FAO/INFOODS Food Composition Table for Western Africa (2019)**.

The copyright statement in the official WAFCT workbook permits copying, downloading and printing with acknowledgement for private study, research, teaching and use in non-commercial products or services. It directs requests for translation and adaptation rights, resale and other commercial use rights to FAO. NutriRoot V0.4 should therefore be treated as a development and evaluation prototype until the intended distribution has been cleared with FAO.
